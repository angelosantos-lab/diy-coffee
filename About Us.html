About DIY Coffee

Born from internet culture—bringing affordable, convenience-store café hacks straight to your cup.

Our Story

DIY Coffee started with a simple idea: you shouldn't have to spend ₱180+ at a specialty café just to enjoy an iced mocha, caramel oat latte, or choco float. Inspired by viral convenience-store drink hacks across the Philippines, we curate easy-to-follow recipes using everyday items from 7-Eleven, Milo, Nescafé, Kopiko, and Oatside.

Whether you're pulling an all-nighter for finals or craving a sweet afternoon pick-me-up, we put the power of being your own barista directly into your hands.

How DIY Coffee Works

01

Grab Your Base

Pick up a classic 7-Eleven Ice Cup and your favorite ready-to-drink coffee or milk.

02

Mix & Customize

Pour, stir, and layer your ingredients—add caramel syrup, oat milk, or a soft-serve cone.

03

Sip & Save

Enjoy a café-worthy drink in under 2 minutes for less than half the price of a coffee shop.
